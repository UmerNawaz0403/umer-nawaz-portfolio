FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes 3 to 5 days, a full website or store 2 to 4 weeks, depending on content and feedback speed.

How do we work across time zones?

I reply within 24 hours and adjust my schedule to overlap with your business hours for calls. Most updates happen over email or chat so nothing gets stuck.

Do I need to provide content and photos?

If you have them, great. If not, I'll guide you on what's needed and help with placeholder content to get started.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Once the project is paid and delivered, the site, code and accounts are fully yours.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every package includes free support after launch. After that, I offer simple hourly or monthly maintenance options.
